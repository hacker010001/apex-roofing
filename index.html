FAQ

How much does a new roof cost?

Most residential replacements fall between $9,000 and $25,000 depending on size, pitch and material. Your inspection gives you an exact, fixed price.

Do you work with insurance?

Yes. We document damage, meet your adjuster on site and handle supplements.

How long does installation take?

Most homes take one to two days, weather permitting.

Are you licensed and insured?

Fully licensed, bonded, with liability and workers' comp. Certificates on request.
